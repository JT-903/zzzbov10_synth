## Preparing solutions

Two options:
- Human concentration: creates 67 umol of target (2.3 mm cube at 100% yield).
    - Dissolve 97 mg of cobalt nitrate *hexahydrate* per mL of water (if anhydrous, 61 mg)
    - Dissolve 46 mg of 1,2,4-triazole per mL of water
    - Dissolve 51 mg of ammonium thiocyanate per mL of water
- More concentrated: dissolve twice as much per mL (confirmed by FIHM group to work) to make twice as much product. Dissolving more is also a possibility.
Make equal volumes of each solution.
Do not forget the antisolvent (ethanol).

Placement:
- cobalt solution: A1
- (trz) solution: A2
- thiocyanate solution: A3
- antisolvent: A4

## Synthesis

Set up deck as in code.
Make sure to triple check everything, and modify reservoir and plate in code/on deck if necessary.
Failure modes:
- Well bottom clearance for (trz) transfer may be far too high. Be ready to stop the procedure, then edit the code to rerun.
- Ethanol is a volatile liquid. Volatile liquids have not been tested yet. Be ready to stop the procedure. DO NOT CONTINUE IF HANDLING IS A PROBLEM - more tests will be necessary.
